Inside the circle

Three places the throw is won.

  1. 01

    The pattern

    Glide or rotational. We pick the entry your body can repeat when the ring feels small and the flight is full, then we clean the first move until it is boringly consistent.

  2. 02

    The power position

    This is where most distance is left on the concrete. A base you can drive from, separation through the middle, and a shot that stays on the neck instead of drifting off the path.

  3. 03

    The release

    A real block, a long arm, and an angle that stays in the sector. The goal is a finish you can trust on the third throw, not only the one that felt good in warmups.

A session

Watch it. Change it. Repeat it.

  1. 01

    Watch

    See the full throw first. Entry, power position, block, and where the shot actually leaves.

  2. 02

    Change

    Fix the one position that gives the most distance back. One cue, drilled until it sticks.

  3. 03

    Repeat

    Take that throw into competitive reps so meet day is a familiar circle, not a new one.
